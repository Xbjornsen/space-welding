Services

Professional welding solutions for residential, commercial, and industrial needs

MIG & TIG Welding

Fast, efficient MIG welding for steel and aluminium projects, plus precision TIG welding for stainless steel, aluminium, and exotic metals. Perfect for high-quality welds on any project.

Flux Core & Stick Welding

Versatile flux-core and stick welding for outdoor and windy conditions. Ideal for heavy-duty structural work, repairs, and construction projects where portability and power are essential.

Custom Fabrication

Bring your ideas to life with custom metalwork. From concept to completion, I create bespoke solutions tailored to your needs.

Repairs & Maintenance

Quick and reliable repairs for damaged metal structures, machinery, and equipment. Get back to work faster with on-site service.

Welding Contracting & Sub-contracting

Taking on full welding contracts for residential, commercial, and industrial projects across Darwin and rural NT — managing the entire scope from planning through to completion. Also available as a sub-contractor to builders and contractors who need a qualified welder on-site.

Mobile Welding

Full welding services at your location. Equipped for on-site work across Darwin, Palmerston, Howard Springs and the rural area — no job too remote!
